The people and principles behind Moldovan table tennis

ATTP is the non-profit organisation that brings together the professional table tennis players, referees and coaches of the Republic of Moldova, under the principles of fair play, integrity and sporting ethics.

Community · Respect · Performance
Who we are

A united force for a growing sport

Founded in 2023 as a free association of players and sports organisations, ATTP exists for a simple reason: table tennis in Moldova deserved a body devoted entirely to the performance of professional players, working alongside the national federation.

Since then the association has run its own national tournament cycle — every day, with its partner clubs — to put the sport back on Moldova’s sporting map and give every player, whatever their age or level, a real chance to compete.

Mission

An environment that helps promote and develop professional table tennis, together with a healthy way of life, across the whole Republic of Moldova.

Vision

Helping everyone who loves this sport make real progress, through a culture of performance built for the long term rather than on one-off results.

Values

Three layers, one game

A paddle is not just a piece of wood: it is a core and two faces working together. The association is built the same way — on three values that do not work apart.
