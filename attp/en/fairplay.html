Sporting integrity

Fair play is not a slogan — it is a condition of taking part

The fair play rules are an integral part of the ATTP Regulations and a mandatory condition for membership of the association and for taking part in any sporting event held under its auspices.

The same rules for everyone

What we expect from every participant

01

Following the rules and regulations of table tennis approved by ATTP.

02

Showing respect to opponents, referees, coaches and everyone else taking part in the event.

03

Competing honestly, with no banned methods or cheating to win.

04

Zero tolerance for any form of corruption or reward for manipulating a sporting result.

Consequences of breaking the rules

Breaching these principles leads to disciplinary sanctions — from disqualification up to expulsion from ATTP and loss of honest-athlete status, with the person’s name placed on the public list of sanctioned individuals. In serious cases it may also lead to criminal or administrative liability.

Why we insist on this

One of ATTP’s core goals, together with its partner sports organisations, is to promote athletes’ integrity and fair play. To that end the association runs educational programmes and awareness campaigns on preventing corruption in sport, in cooperation with the relevant international organisations.
