Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data ATTP processes through this website, for what purpose, and what rights you have. It has been drawn up in accordance with Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection.

1. Who we are

The data controller is the National Association of Professional Table Tennis Players of the Republic of Moldova (ATTP), IDNO 1023620003702, registered at 4 Albișoara St., MD-2001, Chișinău. You can write to us at attp.moldova@gmail.com or call +373 68 650 404.

2. What data we process

Website visitors. The site has no user accounts, uses no traffic analytics and shows no advertising. When you open a page, the servers that deliver the site, its fonts and its technical libraries automatically receive your IP address and browser information, which they need to send you the content.

The contact form. The name, email address, phone number (optional), chosen topic and message you send us.

Players and association members. On player pages we publish name, photo, city, year of birth, category and sporting title, details of playing style and equipment, and rating, ranking position and competition results. For ATTP leadership and staff we publish name, photo and role.

3. Why we process it and on what basis

To reply to you: form data is used only to answer your request. The legal basis is your consent, given by sending the message, and the steps you take before any possible collaboration.

To inform the public about sporting activity: publishing results, rankings and player profiles is part of the association's statutory purpose and of the sporting community's legitimate interest in following competitions. Photos are taken at public competitions.

To deliver the site securely: technical data (IP address, browser) is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the site working and protecting it from abuse.

4. Data sources

Rankings and ratings come from the official ranking of the Table Tennis Federation of the Republic of Moldova, published on the ligas.io platform. Tournament results, match statistics and some player photos come from the Setka Cup platform. This data is imported automatically at regular intervals.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell data or share it for commercial purposes. We use technical providers that process it only on our behalf:

  • GitHub Pages: website hosting;
  • Supabase: the database of players, rankings and news;
  • Web3Forms: forwarding contact form messages to the association's email address;
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: delivering the site's fonts and technical libraries;
  • Google Maps: the map on the contact page, loaded only if you choose to show it.

Some of these providers may process data on servers outside the Republic of Moldova, including in the European Union and the United States. In such cases the transfer takes place with the safeguards required by law, for example the standard contractual clauses these providers apply.

Links to social networks (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Telegram) open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies and local storage

The site uses no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. The only information saved in your browser is strictly technical: a session storage entry (sessionStorage) that lets the site skip the loading animation as you move between pages. It is deleted automatically when you close the tab. The language you choose is part of the page address and is not stored separately.

The Google map on the contact page does not load automatically. Only after you press “Show map” may Google set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy.

7. How long we keep data

We keep contact form messages for as long as needed to reply and for any collaboration that follows, but no longer than 2 years after the last correspondence. Sporting results and rankings are part of competition history and are kept in the association's public archive. A player's profile can be hidden or restricted on request, as described in the next section.

8. Your rights

Under Law No. 195/2024, you have the right to find out what data we hold about you and receive a copy of it, to ask for inaccurate data to be corrected, for your data to be erased or its processing restricted, to object to processing based on legitimate interest (for example the publication of your photo), and to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before that. Where applicable, you also have the right to data portability.

To exercise your rights, write to us at attp.moldova@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days at most. A minor player's data may be managed by their parent or guardian.

If you believe your data is not being processed correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the National Center for Personal Data Protection: 48 Serghei Lazo St., MD-2004, Chișinău, datepersonale.md, centru@datepersonale.md.

9. Security

The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Write access to the database is limited to the site administrators; visitors can only read public information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the site or the law changes. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page. The conditions for using the site are set out separately in the Terms and conditions.
