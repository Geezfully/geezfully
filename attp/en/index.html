Principles

What defines us

Respect

Known in sport as fair play — an unwritten code that shapes who we are, at the table and away from it.

Excellence

Pushing past our own limits, however hard it gets, through determination, discipline and steady courage.

Team

Sport teaches us to treat everyone as equals and to find common goals — that is what makes us an association, not just a list of players.
