Questions

Asked at almost every kitchen table

01 Do you take on small projects?

Occasionally, but honestly not often — we take eight to ten projects a year and they tend to be full rooms or more. If yours is smaller we are glad to point you toward two builders we trust.

02 What does the design agreement cost?

Between $4,500 and $12,000 depending on scope. It buys measured drawings, two or three design schemes and a real construction budget — and it is credited toward the build if you go ahead with us.

03 Can we live in the house?

For a kitchen or a bath, usually yes, with a temporary setup and dust walls. For whole-house work most clients move out for the middle stretch. We will tell you honestly at the design stage.

04 How far ahead are you booking?

Design usually starts within four to six weeks of a signed agreement. Construction starts three to five months out, and the design phase fills most of that gap.

05 Do you work on historic homes?

Most of our work is pre-1940. We handle historic-district review and we know which original details are worth the extra week to save.

06 What happens if you find something behind the wall?

Every project carries a written contingency. When we open something up we photograph it, price the fix and show you the options before anyone touches it.
