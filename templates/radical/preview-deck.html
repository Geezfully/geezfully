Position

We make eight objects a year.

Each is produced in an edition of forty, numbered, and then never made again.

Nothing is designed for a catalogue photograph. Every piece is drawn at full size, built in the workshop, and lived with for a season before it is offered.

Ida Bruun & Anders Holm, founders

How they are made

Four people, one room, no catalogue

  1. Drawn

    Full size, on paper, on the wall

    Nothing is designed on a screen at 40% scale. If it does not work drawn at 1:1 on the workshop wall it does not get made.

  2. Built

    One prototype, lived with

    The first of every edition stays in the studio for a season. Two objects have been abandoned at this stage.

  3. Editioned

    Forty, then never again

    Each is stamped with its number and the year. When the fortieth ships, the tooling is retired.
