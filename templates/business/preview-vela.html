What we are

Three rooms, one fire

The whole building is arranged around a single hearth. Where you sit changes the evening more than the menu does.

01 — Twenty-eight seats

The dining room

One seating from half past five. The kitchen is open to the room, so the fire is the light and the sound of the evening. Courses arrive when they are ready rather than on a clock.

  • Five courses, ninety minutes
  • Counter seats face the fire
  • Wine pairing or by the glass
More about the dining room →

02 — Walk in, stay late

The bar

Ten stools and four high tables, no reservations, open until one. A shorter menu off the same fire, oysters until they run out, and the best seat in the house for eating alone.

  • No booking required
  • Oysters & small plates
  • Open until 1am, Tue–Sat
More about the bar →

03 — Ten to sixteen

The long table

A separate room with its own hearth for groups of ten to sixteen. One menu for the table, chosen with you a week ahead, served family style down the middle.

  • Ten to sixteen guests
  • Menu planned with you
  • Own entrance from the wharf

Before you come

Practical things

01 How do I get a table?

Reservations open thirty days ahead at 9am and go quickly. The bar takes walk-ins every night and is, in our opinion, the better seat.

02 Can you cook around allergies?

Yes, and we would much rather know when you book than when you sit down. A full vegetable menu of the same length is always available with a day’s notice.

03 Is there a dress code?

No. Come from the boat if you like — several people do.

04 Do you take large parties?

Up to sixteen in the long table room, with a menu we plan with you about a week ahead. Write to us rather than using the booking system.

05 Where do we park?

The wharf lot after 5pm is free, and there is a garage two blocks up on Concord. We will validate the garage if you ask on the way out.

06 Are children welcome?

In the dining room until eight, gladly, and the kitchen will happily cook something simple. The bar is over twenty-one after nine.
