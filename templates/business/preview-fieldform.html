01

Meridian Rail — Transport

A rail brand people could actually navigate

2025

BriefFour regional operators merged into one network with four sets of signage, four ticket designs and four apps. Passengers did not know which company they were dealing with, and neither did staff.

What we didOne identity built around a wayfinding system rather than a logo. A typeface drawn for platform legibility at distance, a colour system that codes by line, and a component kit that took the app from nine screens to four.

02

Halo Bio — Biotech

Making twelve years of science legible in one page

2024

BriefA biotech going into a Series C with a story only three people in the building could tell. The deck ran to ninety slides and the website explained the chemistry before it explained the point.

What we didA narrative structure first, identity second. One diagram system for the science, a site that puts the outcome above the mechanism, and a data-visualisation kit their team can extend without us.

03

Ossa — Consumer

A packaging system for 400 SKUs and one shelf

2024

BriefFour hundred products, eleven categories, and a shelf where every one of them competed with the others instead of with the competition.

What we didA grid and colour architecture that reads as one family at three metres and as individual products at thirty centimetres, plus artwork templates their internal team runs without design supervision.
